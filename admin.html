⚡ Bulk import questions

Paste all your questions (or choose a file). They are filled into the form below. Check them, then press Save test. Works with text, JSON, CSV and HTML.

Show formats 
TEXT / HTML (4 options A-D, then the answer):
1. What is the capital of France?
A) Berlin
B) Paris
C) Rome
D) Madrid
Answer: B

(also fine: mark the right option with * or ✓ instead of an Answer line;
 in HTML, a class="correct" on the right option works too)

JSON:
{"title":"Physics Mock 1","subject":"Physics","minutes":30,"class":11,"chapter":"Units and Measurements",
 "questions":[{"q":"2+2?","opts":["3","4","5","6"],"ans":1}]}
(ans: 0-3 or "A"-"D"; keys question/options/answer also work)

CSV (one question per row):
question,A,B,C,D,answer
"Capital of India?",Mumbai,Delhi,Goa,Pune,B
